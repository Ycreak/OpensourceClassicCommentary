How to use this site

Steps
  1. 1
    Paste or upload text

    Select between Paste text check Upload .txt . To upload a .txt file, use Browse files or drag and drop the file.

  2. 2
    Analyse text

    Click Analyse Text to identify possible hidden poetry in prose. This may take a few minutes so please be patient.

  3. 3
    Filter and sort results

    Use the filter panel on the bottom left after selecting wanted filters click Apply .

  4. 4
    Inspect & Sort Results

    Click on a result to expand it. Sort by Meter Text position Distance Confidence Syllable count in either ascending ↑ or descending ↓ order.

Understanding the results

Confidence refers to the model's certainty about a syllable type, confidence of 'iam' is highlighted in the following way:

iam high uncertainty (<50%) iam low confidence (<90%) iam confident

Syllable types are indicated by: for long, for short, and for elisions.

Distance refers to the number of syllables that differ from a known metrical pattern. A meter expects a series of syllable types, and each mismatch increases the distance by 1.

hexameter 0 perfect match hexameter 5 loose match

The short hand of filters on results panel:

DistDistance ConfConfidence SylSyllables EliElisions ICIncomplete meter MonoSylMonosyllabic ending
Filters Explained
  • Maximum distance - chosen edit distance between candidate and known metric patterns.
  • Syllables - min and max syllable count in the candidate.
  • Short / Long syllables - number of syllables in a candidate assigned as either short or long.
  • Elisions - number of syllables in a candidate assigned as elision.
  • Confidence - model certainty over syllable quantity assignments (0–100%).
  • Repeated vowel types - number of distinct vowels appearing more than once in candidate.
  • Incomplete meters - include, exclude, or show only half-line matches.
  • Feet - number of metrical feet in the candidate.
  • Monosyllable as ending - whether the line ends on a monosyllabic word.
